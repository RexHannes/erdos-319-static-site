Formal Backbone

Exact Collision-Energy Decomposition

The formal-backbone checkpoint reports a Lean-proved finite counting identity for ordered subset-sum collisions. If k = |Q| and E_s(Q) counts oriented nonzero reduced signed kernel vectors of support s, then: 

# ordered collisions = 2^k + Σ_s E_s(Q) · 2^(k-s)

The reported theorem is collisionPairs_card_eq_diagonal_add_energy in RequestProject/EnergySpectrumExact.lean. The corresponding rational collision-probability theorem is reported as collisionProbability_eq: 

CP(Q) = (1 + Σ_s E_s(Q) · 2^(-s)) / 2^k

This does not prove the Dominant Short-Energy Conjecture. It gives the energy-spectrum framework a precise finite combinatorial foundation.

Growth-Q Energy Checkpoint

Bounded Support Is False; Energy Compression Survives

The naive hope that primitive smooth reciprocal identities have bounded total support is false. The reported UnboundedSupport.lean milestone proves an all-t cardinality theorem for an infinite family generated from 1/6 = 1/8 + 1/24 and the recursive split 1/n = 1/(2n) + 1/(3n) + 1/(6n).

The corrected framework studies reciprocal subset-sum collision probability through an L1-weighted signed-kernel energy spectrum. A signed kernel vector with support s is exponentially downweighted by 2^{-s}; however, exponentially many long identities could still have macroscopic total weight. The mature target is therefore the Dominant Short-Energy Conjecture.
