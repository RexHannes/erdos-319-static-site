Fixed-Q Update

Sharper But Still Open

Arbitrary fixed Q is now closer, but still open. The new progress is not a full proof, but a sharper ledger: fixed-Q gate theorem architecture, a likely provable sigma_Q joint gate, a prime-only fixed-Q route conditional on Liu-Sawhney, and a reduction of composite/mixed fixed Q to a target-selection / zero-sum / cross-number obstruction problem. Growing Q_N and full #319 remain long-term.

The important correction is explicit: the attempted one-line cross-number closure fails because K(C_n)=1 is false in the needed sense for composite cyclic groups. The C_6 example with cross number 4/3 is preserved as a red-team correction.

We have not solved Erdős #319. The strongest new progress is a fixed-negative-side programme: a Phi-based gate theorem candidate, a joint-avoidance sigma_Q local-mass lemma, and a reduction of arbitrary fixed Q to a finite target-selection problem. The latter now has a precise zero-sum/cross-number formulation. A proposed one-line closure was rejected after finding a cyclic-group counterexample, so the remaining task is sharply localized: verify the Liu-Sawhney reachability interface and prove or classify the reciprocal-realizable atom obstruction. This is genuine progress, but not a completed theorem or a full solution.

Q-Ledger Addendum

Why The Addendum Matters

正面進展被保留下來：fixed-Q gate、sigma_Q joint gate、prime-only fixed Q、target-selection route 都被整理成明確 proof tasks，而不是散亂 brainstorm。

錯誤也被正式記錄：cross-number 的 one-line closure 被 C6 counterexample 擊破，所以 addendum 防止我們誤稱 arbitrary fixed Q 已解。

剩下問題變清楚了：現在不是 vague gap，而是幾個精確缺口 - Liu-Sawhney interface、composite target-selection、reciprocal-realizable atom cross-number bound、以及 growing Q_N 的 uniformity。

一句話：addendum 的價值不是宣稱解了 #319，而是把 arbitrary fixed Q 從模糊障礙變成一組可審核、可計算、可 formalize 的具體問題。
